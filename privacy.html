Privacy

Bigfoot Truck Beds LLC, Mulberry, Florida. Updated November 2026.

What we collect

When you ask for a quote on this website or through one of our Facebook or Instagram forms, we get what you type in: your name, phone number, and if you give them, your email, city or ZIP, your truck and any notes.

What we use it for

Only to call, text or email you back about a truck bed you asked about. That's it.

Who sees it

The people at Bigfoot Truck Beds who answer quote requests. We do not sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with anyone else for their marketing.

How it's kept

Quote requests are stored on a private server and sent to our team by email. Ask and we'll delete yours.

Visit counts and cookies

We count visits to this website, and taps on the call and quote buttons, so we know how the site is doing. That count uses no cookies and does not keep your IP address.

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. If that changes, this page will say so.
